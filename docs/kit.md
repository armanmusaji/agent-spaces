# The review kit

The conventions that made two agents' work reviewable by one person in a design file. These are documented conventions with examples, not a component download. They came out of Arman's walkthroughs during the trial, numbered as they were adopted. Additions to the wider playbook are proposed until its next version is approved.

1. **Thread authorship.** The tool shows the human's name on every comment an agent posts. Convention: the first message in a thread is the agent's opener; every reply is the human's unless it starts with the agent's name in brackets. Agents keep thread text short; substance lives on the canvas.
2. **Open threads.** Each space's header card carries one open thread for that space, and the orientation card carries one for the whole project. The human writes anything there; agents read all open threads at the start of every session.
3. **Orientation.** A Start here card at the top left: what the file is, the two kinds of users (the designer running the agents, and the fictional end user of the task), the task, the page layout, how to review, where things stand and what needs the human. Each space begins with a header card: direction name, the bet, status, what needs a ruling.
4. **Rationale per screen.** Callouts are numbered per screen starting at 1. Each screen has its own legend directly under it, the same width as the screen. Nothing about a screen lives anywhere else.
5. **Never delete a commented object.** Once the human has pinned a comment to something, it is moved to a Set aside area, never removed. Learned when three frames were deleted during a rework and the comments on them lost their anchors.
6. **Readable at fit zoom.** Canvas notes use their own type scale (title 36, heading 26, body 22, label 20, caption 17), never the screen's, and sit in narrow columns: orientation 900 px, space header 760 px, per-screen legend 500 px. Test: fit the whole section on screen and read the note without zooming.
7. **A grid for a space.** Columns of 550 px: screen 390, callout lane 120 (callouts start 8 px right of the screen edge), gutter 40. Screens share one top edge. Each legend is 500 px wide, flush with its screen's left edge, 32 px below it. Nothing overlaps a neighbor.
8. **Agent notes look nothing like the product.** Every note sits on a dark surface with light text, a 3 px border in the author's color, and its own fonts (a grotesque for titles and body, a mono for tags and captions). Screens stay in the product's own type and colors. Author colors: Claude amber, Astra blue.

Two conventions the prototype adds on top of the kit:

- **A needs-you view.** One list of every open decision, who is waiting, and one tap to the object. The summary counts open and deferred items separately and says nothing needs you only when both are zero.
- **A Pick step.** Develop, review, pick, ruling. After the first review the director chooses one direction to build on.
